Harbour WardTheme · edited 12 minutes ago

Harbour Ward runs along the eastern waterfront, from the Customs House to the ferry steps. Most of the city's trade passes through it at least once, and much of that trade is argued over in its taverns before it is ever unloaded.

Geography

The ward is built on reclaimed land. Its streets drop toward the water in a series of short, steep terraces, and the lowest of them flood at spring tide. Residents measure a building's age by how many times its doorstep has been raised.

The Lower Docks

The busiest stretch of quay, and the one the Guild of Lamplighters lights first each evening. The Salt Market sets up here at dusk.

Notable residents

Tomas Reyes has walked the quay lamps for eleven winters. Magister Oake keeps rooms above the Customs House and a pew at the Tidewater Chapel.
