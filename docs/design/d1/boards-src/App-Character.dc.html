History

Lean and quiet, in a lamplighter's long coat buttoned to the throat. Ink stains on the fingers of his right hand, from the Guild's route ledgers he insists on keeping himself.

Tomas has walked the quay lamps of Harbour Ward for eleven winters. He owes Ilsa Varn two quarters' dues at the Salt Market and has never once been late with an excuse.

Habits

Lights the Lower Docks first, always, and the Ferry Steps last. Plays cards badly with Wren Halloway on the steps after the last lamp is lit.
