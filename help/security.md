# Security

SharpMUSH decides who may do what in a few layers:

- Roles and permissions decide what an account, its characters and their objects may do.
  WIZARD, ROYALTY and the powers are roles too. See [roles] and [administrative capabilities].
- Locks decide who may use, enter, pass or otherwise act on one object. See [@lock] and [locktypes].
- Command and function restrictions narrow what the whole game may use. See [restrict].
- Site locks decide which addresses may connect, create characters or register accounts.
  See [@sitelock].
